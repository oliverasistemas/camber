1.3 Camber ID normalization
  - Problem: Vervent creates separate deficiency contracts for one lease,
    which fragments the data.
  - Solution: consolidate all contracts of a lease into a single view using
    a normalized ID.
  - Formula to apply:

      =LET(x,TRIM([@LEASE]&""),IF(NOT(ISNUMBER(--RIGHT(x,1))),LEFT(x,1)&"0"&MID(x,2,LEN(x)-2),x))

  - What it does: trims the lease ID; if the last character is not a digit,
    it drops that last character and inserts a "0" after the first character.
    Otherwise the ID is left as is.
  - Fathom describes it as: remove the "D" and add a leading "0"
    (D1234 -> 01234). That does not match the formula; see OPEN QUESTIONS.

4. TERMINATIONS AND TERMINATION REPORT
--------------------------------------
4.1 Manheim Market Report (MMR) values
  - MMR value at T-30, T-60 and T-90 days.
  - Future: integrate the Manheim API for automated MMR values (at lease
    signing, 12 months ago, today, and at maturity).

4.2 Vehicle status progression
  - Vehicle status at T-90, T-60, T-30 and current, with the columns in that
    order.
  - Possible statuses:
      With Lessee - Original Term
      With Lessee - Extension
      With Lessee - Post Maturity
      Repossession
      In Inventory
      Theft / Unrecovered
      Sold

4.3 Residual loss forecasting
  - Expected gain/loss on the vehicle sale.
  - Formula: Contract Residual Value - MMR Value.
  - Contract Residual Value comes from Camber's lease tape (not Vervent) and
    must be manually overrideable.

4.4 Lessee outreach tracking (future)
  - Camber's notes: Camber will contact the lessee to ask about their intent
    with the lease; the intent is recorded with the date of the contact, and
    the report shows the recorded intent at T-30, T-60 and T-90 days.
  - Fathom: track whether Vervent contacted the lessee at 180, 120, 90, 60
    and 30 days before maturity. A date in the field means the contact
    occurred; blank means it did not.
  - Later: track the lessee's latest recorded intent (e.g. Buy, Return,
    Undecided).
  - Who contacts and at which checkpoints differ; see OPEN QUESTIONS.

4.5 Lessee payoff scenarios (Camber calculations)
  - Purpose: identify lessees who could lower their monthly payment by
    purchasing the vehicle.
  - Required fields:
      Total Payoff Amount
      Loan-to-Value (LTV)
      New Monthly Payment (with a loan)

4.6 Lease termination scenarios (Camber calculations)
  - Termination Date: manual entry field.
  - Prepayment boolean:
      Adolfo's notes: based on whether the car was repossessed or not; Brad
      will provide the full formula.
      Fathom: a lease terminated more than 90 days before maturity is a
      prepayment.
    See OPEN QUESTIONS.
  - Remaining payments: number and dollar value of the remaining payments at
    termination.

4.7 Present value calculations
  - Purpose: remove the interest portion from the remaining payments to get
    the true principal owed, in case the vehicle is returned by the lessee.
  - Method: present-value the remaining payments and the contract residual
    value at the lessor's yield.
  - Formula for the remaining payments:

      =IF([@[Remaining Lease Payments as of Termination Date ($)]]="","",[@[Remaining Lease Payments as of Termination Date ($)]]/(1+[@[Lease_DCF]]*(DAYS([@[maturity_date]],[@[Termination Date]])/2)/365))

  - What it does: blank if there are no remaining payments; otherwise
    remaining payments / (1 + Lease_DCF * (days from termination date to
    maturity date / 2) / 365). That is a simple-interest discount at the
    Lease_DCF rate over half the remaining term (the average time until the
    payments would have been received).
  - Lease_DCF is the lessor's yield.
