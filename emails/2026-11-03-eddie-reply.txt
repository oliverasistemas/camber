Thanks for the matrix. We reviewed 1277877 and the other recent counters
against V1.2 and have the picture below. We are confirming the remaining
details with Karus before Wednesday.

What we found:

1. Valuation. The engine computes LTV on the wholesale (trade) value, as you
   observed. Your matrix uses NADA Retail. This is a program setting, not a
   calculation error, and it affects every callback: three of the five recent
   counters were cut to exactly 130% of wholesale.

2. Term. The engine's maximum term is 75 months, stepping down with vehicle
   age and mileage. That is why 1277877 came back at 70 and others at 60–72.
   The 84-month term for amounts over $25,000 is not in the current program.

3. DTI on the 84-month resubmit. We pulled the engine logs for all five
   submissions of 1277877. Nothing in the application changed between the
   10/29 counter and the 11/2 declines except the requested term (70 to
   83/84) and the 0.49% rate add-on. The 84-month payment is about $700,
   below the $784 that was countered, so DTI did not get worse. The engine
   is hard-declining a term above its current 75-month cap and labeling it
   DTI instead of countering to the cap. We have raised it with Karus as a
   defect.

   The $62 reduction on the 10/29 counter was the engine's 15% PTI cap
   (your matrix allows 20% for a 717 score), and the engine valued the
   truck at the $33,550 wholesale figure you sent, not the $37,550 retail.

4. Callbacks return a single term (min and max are the same), so "approved up
   to 84 months" is not something the current response can express. We are
   checking whether a term range can be returned.

Beyond those, the matrix and the program differ on credit tiers (V1.2 has
six bands starting at 740; the engine has five starting at 700), PTI, minimum
income, maximum payment, maximum amount financed, mileage (130k vs. 120k,
still open from Kim's 10/22 notes) and several credit-history rules. We will
bring the full side-by-side to the meeting so we can agree on which set the
program should run and get it changed.
