@Adolfo Olivera

It’s a mix of the two. A fixed set applies to every Camber deal: driver’s license, ID, proof of residence, proof of insurance and references. Employment verification, residence verification and the trade-in requirement are meant to depend on the application. Employment and residence verification have been firing on every deal because our parser wasn’t reading the address and employer from the TU report, so there was nothing to compare the application against. Since these are refinances, I believe the trade-in requirement should be removed. I’ll get these changes in, thanks for catching this.
Right now POR is on every Camber deal; it’s part of the fixed set. We receive the driver’s license number and state but not the address on the license, so we can’t make that comparison ourselves right now. However, we can check the application address against the address on the TU report, which is what the residence verification stip will do once the parser fix is in. To require POR only when the license and application addresses differ, we need the license address. Alternatively, we could also word the stip so the dealer checks it at funding, e.g. “Proof of residence needed if driver’s license address does not match application address.”
Neither is tied to FICO, PTI or DTI today. Employment verification is triggered when the employer on the TU report doesn’t match the employer on the application, or when TU has no employer on file. Proof of income is meant to be triggered when stated income is more than 10% above TransUnion’s income estimate, but a setting has kept it from ever firing. I’ll fix that alongside the parser change.
