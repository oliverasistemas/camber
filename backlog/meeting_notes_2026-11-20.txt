2.7 Remaining payments if the vehicle is returned by the lessee
  - Total dollar value of the remaining payments.
  - NPV of the remaining payments, using the lessor's yield.
  - Formula:

      =IF([@[Remaining Lease Payments as of Termination Date ($)]]="","",[@[Remaining Lease Payments as of Termination Date ($)]]/(1+[@[Lease_DCF]]*(DAYS([@[maturity_date]],[@[Termination Date]])/2)/365))

  - What it does: blank if there are no remaining payments; otherwise
    remaining payments / (1 + Lease_DCF * (days from termination date to
    maturity date / 2) / 365). That is a simple-interest discount at the
    Lease_DCF rate over half the remaining term (the average time until the
    payments would have been received).
  - Lease_DCF is the lessor's yield.
