to Russell, Zoe, me, Benjamin

Hi all,

Attached is an initial draft of the Master Servicing template for lease terminations. The template includes the information we need to track for:
Vehicle statuses
Pre-lease termination outreach to record customer intent (return vehicle, purchase, etc.)
Lease termination dates/scenarios and potential amounts due at lease termination for remaining lease payments, the contract residual, past-due balances, and PPT
Vehicle returns and inspections, including documenting vehicle condition, excess mileage/wear fees, disposition fees, and MMR values
Billing at lease termination to calculate the amounts due based on the lease termination type and when the invoices were sent to the customer
Vehicle sales, including calculating any residual shortfalls due following the sale and when the invoices were sent to the customer
Collections and accounts receivable, including tracking of any amounts waived or charged off
The date that the title was released following the vehicle sale
The template also includes a sample data override layer that we should create to correct values in our reports that are validated through a different source. Example: Camber lease tape lists an annual mileage allowance of 15k mi/year, but the lease agreement states 12k mi/year.

I am also working on templates for the dashboards to sit on top of this data, but I wanted to start by defining the template for what is needed to effectively manage lease terminations.

Please review and let me know if you have any immediate feedback or questions. I will also schedule time for us to review the template in more detail.

Thank you,
Brad
